Who We Are

Honoring the ones who cared for us all.

A nurse honor guard is a group of nurses who volunteer to pay tribute to their deceased colleagues. It is a ceremonial acknowledgment of the nurse's contribution and dedication to the profession.

The Heart of Appalachia Nurse Honor Guard was put together by Joy Goins to honor the lives, service and dedication of nurses who devoted themselves to caring for others, and to support grieving families. We serve the Tri-State area from Bell County, Kentucky.

We depend on the community. If you know a nurse who is ill or has passed away, please tell us.

The Tribute

What a tribute looks like

Nurse honor guards across the country commonly perform the Nightingale Tribute, named for Florence Nightingale. Details vary from guard to guard and family to family.

1

The lamp

A Nightingale lamp is lit for the nurse's years of dedication.

2

The white rose

Placed near the casket or urn.

3

The final call

The nurse's name is called and a triangle is rung three times.
